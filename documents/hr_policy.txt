DSNK Technologies - HR Policy (Version 2.0, effective January 2026)

1. Working Hours
- Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break.
- Flexible timing is allowed between 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM start, with manager approval.

2. Work From Home (WFH)
- Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.
- Full remote work requires approval from the department head and HR.

3. Leave Policy
- Casual Leave: 12 days per year
- Sick Leave: 10 days per year (medical certificate needed for more than 2 consecutive days)
- Earned/Privilege Leave: 18 days per year; up to 30 days can be carried forward
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks paid
- Paternity Leave: 10 working days paid
- Public Holidays: 12 days per year as per the published holiday calendar
- Leave must be applied through the HR portal at least 3 days in advance (except sick leave).

4. Probation
- New employees have a 6-month probation period.
- Notice period during probation: 15 days. After confirmation: 60 days.

5. Salary and Payroll
- Salary is credited on the last working day of every month.
- Annual performance appraisal happens in April.

6. Benefits
- Health insurance of INR 5,00,000 for employee, spouse, and two children.
- Learning allowance of INR 20,000 per year for courses and certifications.
- Employee referral bonus: INR 25,000 after the referred candidate completes 3 months.

7. Code of Conduct
- Treat colleagues and clients with respect.
- Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination. Report issues to hr@dsnktech.example.com.
- Protect confidential company and client data.

8. Dress Code
- Business casual Monday to Thursday; casual on Fridays.
